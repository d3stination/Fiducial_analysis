**Question:** Which definitions fail, and are failures missed, invalid or upstream-ineligible?

**Dataset/sample:** 17 participants, 212 recordings and 1706 beats; 22178 eligible attempted method-beat records.

**Source data:** [F04_HeartCycle_X_detection_failure.csv](fig04_heartcycle_x_detection_failure.csv) and the canonical tables identified in the calculation.

**Calculation:** The primary estimate is each participant's missed + invalid percentage, summarised by its median and participant-bootstrap 95% CI. The open diamond is the secondary pooled beat-weighted total. The right panel separates pooled missed/attempted, invalid/attempted and upstream-ineligible/all-record rates on one explicitly labelled symmetric-log scale; the left panel retains every uncertainty interval on an unclipped linear scale.

**Axes:** Y: X-point algorithm definition. Left X: participant missed + invalid rate (%). Right X: pooled status-specific rate (%), nonlinear only outside ±0.25%.

**Result:** The largest pooled attempted-failure rate was X4 in Overall: 0 missed + 834 invalid of 1706 attempts (48.89%); 0 additional records were upstream-ineligible and excluded from that denominator.

**Interpretation:** The participant estimate is primary; pooled totals and status composition show whether a similar percentage arises from different execution outcomes.

**Limitation:** Failure is not timing accuracy. The right-panel nonlinear scale expands rare statuses but does not change their values; upstream ineligibility is contextual and remains distinct from attempted algorithm failure.

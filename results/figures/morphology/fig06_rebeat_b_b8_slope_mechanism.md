**Question:** Which measured waveform characteristic is associated with method-specific localisation error, failure or disagreement?

**Dataset/sample:** ReBeat, Cognitive workload, B-point method B8; 24 estimable participants and 8261 available method-beat observations.

**Source data:** [F06_ReBeat_B_B8_slope_mechanism.csv](fig06_rebeat_b_b8_slope_mechanism.csv) and the canonical tables identified in the calculation.

**Calculation:** The complete six-feature/all-method audit selected this display without changing the frozen method. Outcome: method-specific peer disagreement. Feature: Local waveform slope. Participant effect: within-participant Spearman rho; minimum data rule: at least 8 paired observations with variation in feature and outcome. The two waveforms are actual low/high-feature or valid/failed examples from the plotted sample.

**Axes:** Quantitative panel — X: Within-participant Spearman rho: local waveform slope vs absolute difference from other-method median (ms); Y: participant ID plus the participant-median effect and bootstrap 95% CI.

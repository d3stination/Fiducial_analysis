# Figure 6 selected participant-aware morphology effects
**Dataset/sample:** HeartCycle B1 uses 17 participants and 986 strict common-reference method-beat pairs. IITB post-exercise X4 uses 16 estimable participants and 1,610 attempted method-beat records with feature values.

**Result:** HeartCycle B1 median rho 0.216 (95% interval 0.117 to 0.305; q=0.0237). IITB X4 failed-minus-valid sharpness contrast -460 curvature units (95% interval -1,002 to -381; q=0.00525).

**Limitation:** These participant-aware associations are exploratory and do not establish causation. The two panels use different datasets, outcomes, denominators and feature units, so their effect sizes are not directly comparable.


The left panel shows one HeartCycle participant-level Spearman coefficient for B1 local slope versus absolute ECHO AVO timing error. The right panel shows one IITB post-exercise participant-level failed-minus-valid sharpness contrast for X4. Diamonds and bars are the participant median and bootstrap 95% interval. B1 had a median rho of 0.216 (95% interval 0.117 to 0.305; q=0.0237; n=17). X4 failed beats had lower sharpness by a median 460 dataset-specific curvature units (95% interval -1,002 to -381; q=0.00525; n=16).

**Question:** How much does the same participant change from normal to stress?

**Dataset/sample:** 24 matched participants with both conditions; all-method range.

**Source data:** [F07_ReBeat_X_within_beat_range_change.csv](fig07_rebeat_x_within_beat_range_change.csv) and the canonical tables identified in the calculation.

**Calculation:** Signed participant change = stress condition minus normal condition, using paired participant summaries only.

**Axes:** Y: participant ID. X: Median timing-range change (ms); zero indicates no paired change.

**Result:** Median paired change was 15.59 (ms; participant-bootstrap 95% CI 4.00 to 20.00); 17/24 participants increased.

**Interpretation:** The signed display makes direction and participant consistency explicit beyond the two-value Figure 5A view.

**Limitation:** Timing-range change is across-method disagreement rather than independent-reference accuracy.

**RQ1 contribution:** Quantifies within-person condition sensitivity without mixing unmatched participants.

**Question:** How much does the same participant change from normal to stress?

**Dataset/sample:** 24 matched participants with both conditions; selected method X4.

**Source data:** [F07_ReBeat_X_failure_rate_change.csv](fig07_rebeat_x_failure_rate_change.csv) and the canonical tables identified in the calculation.

**Calculation:** Signed participant change = stress condition minus normal condition, using paired participant summaries only.

**Axes:** Y: participant ID. X: Failure-rate change (percentage points); zero indicates no paired change.

**Result:** Median paired change was -1.77 (percentage points; participant-bootstrap 95% CI -6.59 to 1.66); 10/24 participants increased.

**Interpretation:** The signed display makes direction and participant consistency explicit beyond the two-value Figure 5A view.

**Limitation:** The failure panel represents X4, not all definitions; timing-range change remains disagreement rather than accuracy.

**RQ1 contribution:** Quantifies within-person condition sensitivity without mixing unmatched participants.

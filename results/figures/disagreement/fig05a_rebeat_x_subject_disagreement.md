**Question:** Which participants have greater across-method timing disagreement?

**Dataset/sample:** 24 participants; 8951 participant-summed common-valid beats across displayed conditions.

**Source data:** [F05A_ReBeat_X_subject_disagreement.csv](fig05a_rebeat_x_subject_disagreement.csv) and the canonical tables identified in the calculation.

**Calculation:** For each common-valid beat, X range = latest minus earliest of all 13 methods; the displayed point is each participant-condition median. Vertical solid/dotted lines are the across-participant median/P95, not within-person error bars.

**Axes:** Y: participant ID. X: participant median within-beat X-point timing range (ms).

**Result:** The median paired change was 15.59 ms (participant-bootstrap 95% CI 4.00 to 20.00); 17/24 participants increased and 7/24 decreased.

**Interpretation:** Participant trajectories show whether a condition effect is consistent or driven by a subset of people.

**Limitation:** Across-method range measures consensus, not physiological correctness; it is defined only on all-method valid beats.

**RQ1 contribution:** Separates subject heterogeneity from a pooled change in algorithm disagreement.

**Question:** Which definitions select positions most different from the other methods?

**Dataset/sample:** 17 participants and 852 participant-summed common-valid beats across conditions; each participant estimate uses identical all-method common-valid beats.

**Source data:** [F05B_HeartCycle_X_definition_disagreement.csv](fig05b_heartcycle_x_definition_disagreement.csv) and the canonical tables identified in the calculation.

**Calculation:** For every common-valid beat, absolute difference = |target method − median(other methods)|; each neutral small point is one participant, vertically spread deterministically within its condition lane. Condition-specific diamonds and horizontal bars are across-participant medians and IQRs.

**Axes:** Y: X-point algorithm definition. X: participant median absolute peer difference (ms).

**Result:** The largest across-participant median was X10 in Overall (144.16 ms).

**Interpretation:** Large values identify definitions that operationalise a different waveform location.

**Limitation:** Divergence is not inaccuracy, and common-valid restriction can omit difficult beats.

**RQ1 contribution:** Identifies which mathematical definitions contribute most to localisation variability.

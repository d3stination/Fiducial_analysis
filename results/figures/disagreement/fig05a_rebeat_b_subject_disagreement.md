**Question:** Which participants have greater across-method timing disagreement?

**Dataset/sample:** 24 participants; 16536 participant-summed common-valid beats across displayed conditions.

**Source data:** [F05A_ReBeat_B_subject_disagreement.csv](fig05a_rebeat_b_subject_disagreement.csv) and the canonical tables identified in the calculation.

**Calculation:** For each common-valid beat, B range = latest minus earliest of all 10 methods; the displayed point is each participant-condition median. Vertical solid/dotted lines are the across-participant median/P95, not within-person error bars.

**Axes:** Y: participant ID. X: participant median within-beat B-point timing range (ms).

**Result:** The median paired change was 0.86 ms (participant-bootstrap 95% CI -0.41 to 2.05); 16/24 participants increased and 8/24 decreased.

**Interpretation:** Participant trajectories show whether a condition effect is consistent or driven by a subset of people.

**Limitation:** Across-method range measures consensus, not physiological correctness; it is defined only on all-method valid beats.

**RQ1 contribution:** Separates subject heterogeneity from a pooled change in algorithm disagreement.

**Question:** Do the same frozen definitions show similar failure patterns across recording environments?

**Dataset/sample:** HeartCycle overall (17 participants), IITB rest (18), and ReBeat baseline (24); acquisition/reference differences are tabulated in RQ1_dataset_comparability.csv.

**Source data:** [F08_cross_dataset_X_detection_failure.csv](fig08_cross_dataset_x_detection_failure.csv) and the canonical tables identified in the calculation.

**Calculation:** Failure = 100 × (missed + invalid) / eligible attempts. Circles/bars are participant medians/IQRs and open diamonds pooled rates. All upper panels share the honest 0–81% scale; every lower panel repeats the same values over the identical 0–5% magnified scale. No dataset-specific scale is used and no value is removed from the complete view.
